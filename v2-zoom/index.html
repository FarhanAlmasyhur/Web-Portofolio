    January 2025 to now, Singapore

    Seeing inside concrete

    At Screening Eagle Dreamlab I work on the Profometer. An engineer rolls a probe across a concrete slab, the probe senses the steel inside, and it streams what it finds over Bluetooth to an iPad. The app draws where every bar is and how deep it sits. The PM8000 finds the steel; the PM8500 maps where it's corroding.

    I write that iPad app, next to the people who build the hardware: custom measurement drawing in UIKit, state kept predictable with ReSwift, and a Bluetooth link that holds steady while data streams in. Engineers in more than 20 countries use it.
